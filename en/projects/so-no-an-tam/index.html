An Tâm

Context

Personal debt tracking benefits from having loans and payment schedules in one place. An Tâm was built as a tool for organizing personal debts.

Approach

Information is grouped into an overview, debts, a journal, a calendar and reports. Visitors can explore a sample-data demo.

Work & evidence

The application is complete and publicly available. Demo balances are sample data; user counts and financial outcomes have not been published.
