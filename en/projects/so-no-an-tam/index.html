01 · THE IDEA

Make room in your mind by putting the numbers in one place

A loan in a message, a due date on a calendar, a payment in a notebook: scattered details make it hard to see the full picture. An Tâm brings debts, due dates and payments together. It does not judge or promise to make debt disappear; it helps you see what has been paid, what remains and what needs attention.

03 · CALM BY DESIGN

Small steps deserve to be visible

Gentle greens, breathing room and clear information hierarchy make difficult numbers easier to face. Progress bars and donut charts place repayments alongside remaining balances. A correctly recorded payment becomes a visible step forward, not another reason for self-judgment.

The behavioral principle: less to hold in working memory means more attention for one concrete action: verify a debt, check a date or log a payment. Visibility may support consistent habits, but repayment outcomes still depend on real finances and agreements.

04 · SECURITY & RECOVERY

Privacy means distinct protective layers

01

Encrypted vault

Financial data is encrypted on-device with AES-256-GCM and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 key derivation. The server stores ciphertext rather than requiring your vault password. Manual and idle locking are available.

02

Email & account password

Email-based registration, sign-in and account-recovery requests are supported. Recovering the account does not decrypt the vault: its separate password must still be kept safely.

03

Brave Sync Chain (browser option)

Brave users may choose to sync browser data such as saved passwords. Brave Sync does not sync debt records or restore the vault key; it is not the application's own debt-sync system.

04

Device transfer & limited sharing

Encrypted JSON exports support transferring data while retaining the vault password. Shared reports are selected snapshots with expiry, password and revocation controls, not full vault access.

Important distinction: your account password authenticates access, your vault password decrypts private data, and Brave Sync is an optional browser feature. Keep a safe copy of your encrypted backup and your vault credentials.

05 · EMAIL + BRAVE SYNC CHAIN

How do email, the encrypted vault and Brave Sync work together?

Think of three independent protections working side by side: email identifies your account; the app encrypts and synchronizes the vault’s ciphertext; Brave’s optional Sync chain keeps selected browser information consistent across your Brave devices.

TWO DISTINCT SYNCHRONIZATION PATHSSelect each step to explore
APPLICATION · EMAIL + ENCRYPTED VAULT
01 · Email sign-in: Email sign-in identifies the correct app account. Recovery emails can help restore account access, not the vault decryption key.
02 · Open local vault: The user enters a distinct vault secret; the application derives the key on the device. Email access alone cannot reveal the records.
03 · Encrypt records: Debt records are encrypted locally. The app’s server receives the encrypted payload over HTTPS, not the plaintext or the vault password.
04 · Read on another device: Sign in to the same app account to retrieve its encrypted data, then unlock locally with the vault secret. This path belongs to An Tâm, not Brave Sync.
BRAVE BROWSER · OPTIONAL SYNC CHAIN
Brave · Device A
↔ Brave Sync Chain ↔
Brave · Device B

Brave Sync can optionally carry browser items such as saved passwords, bookmarks and settings, as selected in Brave. It does not synchronize An Tâm’s debt database or supply the vault decryption key.

ⓘ Important: resetting an email login does not reset the vault secret. Brave Sync is not an application backup, and its Sync code should be protected separately. Keep an encrypted backup when available.

07 · HOW TO BEGIN

Three manageable steps

  1. Record accurately: add creditors, balances and due dates; flag anything that needs verification.
  2. Choose your next action: review your schedule and pick a realistic payment based on your circumstances.
  3. Document progress: record each transaction, review trends and keep an encrypted backup.

An Tâm is a personal information organizer. It does not automatically transfer funds, settle bank debts or replace creditor agreements and professional financial advice. The demo contains sample data.

START WITH ONE SMALL STEP

You do not have to solve everything today

Start by understanding one debt clearly. The next steps are easier when your information has a place to live.
